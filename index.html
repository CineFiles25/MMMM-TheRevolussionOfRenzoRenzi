The Revolussion of Renzo Renzi

Renzo Renzi was a writer, critic and filmmaker who worked in Bologna. This virtual exhibition follows his life and work through sixteen objects: letters, manuscripts, photographs, drawings, a book, a film and a recording.

How to visit

The museum has three rooms, and you can start in any of them. Begin in room 1 to meet Renzo as a young man, then continue to the filmmaker in room 2 and the friend in room 3. The map shows each room with a short introduction.

  1. Choose a narrative: a route through the objects, such as Renzi's life in date order.
  2. Open an item and read it in the version you prefer: read less, read more or told by Renzo.
  3. Move from one item to the next with the buttons at the bottom of the page.
