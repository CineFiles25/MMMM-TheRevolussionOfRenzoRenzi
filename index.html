Three narratives

Take a journey through the life and work of Renzo Renzi, exploring his films, writings, and the city of Bologna.

Renzo Renzi: a life

Guide to Renzo Renzi's life and work, told in date order: from his life as a son and soldier to that as a filmmaker and writer.

From subject to film

Follow La strada from the page to the screen and beyond. Renzi's 1969 book of Fellini screenplays shows how a film story can also be read. A drawing and two production stills then take Gelsomina out of the film frame, to the soundtrack, the finished film and its premiere at Cinema Fulgor in Bologna. Each stop moves the story further from the script and closer to an audience.

Guide to walking in Bologna

A walk around Renzi's city, Bologna, through Renzi's eyes, words and film camera. Five stops offer five ways of meeting the city: walk it, film it, read it, watch a film in it, and remember it.

How to visit

  1. Choose a narrative above, or open the map to start in a room.
  2. Open an item and read it in the version you prefer: read less, read more or told by Renzo.
  3. Move from one item to the next with the buttons at the bottom of the page.
